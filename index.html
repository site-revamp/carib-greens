The Carib Greens Perspective

Where clean energy meets smart agriculture.

Food production is evolving. Advances in controlled environments, automation and renewable energy are creating new ways to think about how food can be grown, managed and supplied.

Carib Greens is positioned around this intersection of agriculture and technology, with a Caribbean-focused vision for intelligent, sustainable indoor farming.

The ambition is to bring growing systems, energy thinking and agricultural intelligence into one connected approach.

A Caribbean-Focused Approach

Designed around the region's possibilities.

The Caribbean is the central focus of the Carib Greens vision. Rather than treating indoor farming as a one-size-fits-all idea, the approach starts with the importance of regional context, practical deployment and the needs of communities.

Specific operating countries, facilities and deployment locations should be confirmed as the project develops.
